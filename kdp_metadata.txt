<h2>Color. Learn. Connect with Nature.</h2>

<p><strong>Backyard Birds of North America — Volume 1</strong> combines the meditative joy of adult coloring with the depth of a real field guide. Forty of the continent's most beloved birds, each presented across three sections: two richly detailed educational pages and one full-page coloring illustration.</p>

<h3>What's inside each of the 40 bird modules:</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Natural history profile: habitat &amp; range, diet, behavior, nesting, song, and conservation status</li>
  <li>Quick Stats box: length, wingspan, weight, and lifespan in imperial and metric</li>
  <li>IUCN Red List status and Breeding Bird Survey population trend data</li>
  <li>Practical feeder &amp; garden tips for attracting the species</li>
  <li>A color guide identifying every key plumage zone with suggested colors</li>
  <li>A full-page coloring illustration — printed single-sided on a blank-backed page so markers never bleed through</li>
</ul>

<h3>40 featured species include:</h3>
<p>American Goldfinch • Northern Cardinal • Black-capped Chickadee • Blue Jay • Ruby-throated Hummingbird • Eastern Bluebird • Baltimore Oriole • Cedar Waxwing • American Kestrel • Steller's Jay • Northern Flicker • Downy Woodpecker • Hermit Thrush • American Robin • Rose-breasted Grosbeak • Common Yellowthroat • and 24 more</p>

<h3>Designed for:</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Adult colorists seeking a meaningful, nature-themed project</li>
  <li>Backyard birders who want to deepen their knowledge of common species</li>
  <li>Educators and naturalists looking for an engaging reference tool</li>
  <li>Gift-givers seeking a thoughtful present for nature and art lovers</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>168 pages • 8.5 × 11 inches • Professional lay-flat binding</strong></p>
<p>Works beautifully with colored pencils, watercolor pencils, and fine-tip markers. Each coloring page is printed on a single side with a blank reverse — no bleed-through, no worries.</p>
